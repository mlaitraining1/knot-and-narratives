The studio

Most photographers arrive on the wedding day. We're already in the room.

The Knot & Narratives began with a simple, slightly stubborn idea: the way you move on your wedding day — down the aisle, into your first dance, through the mandap — shapes every photograph taken of it. So instead of hiring a photographer and a choreographer separately and hoping they sync up, our couples get both under one roof, working from one shared shot list, one timeline, one story.

The result is footage and photography that looks less staged and more like it was always going to happen exactly that way.
