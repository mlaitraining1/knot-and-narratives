The sangeet number the bride swore she'd never agree to

Six weeks before the wedding, Ritika sent us a voice note that started with "absolutely not" and ended, forty seconds later, with "okay fine, but nothing where I have to lift my arms above my head." What followed was a choreography brief built entirely around what she was comfortable with — which, it turns out, is the only kind of brief that ever actually works on the night.
