Two crafts, one photographer who refused to pick.

The studio behind The Knot & Narratives, and the slightly stubborn idea it was built on.

Founder & lead photographer

Kabir Anand

Kabir trained as a dancer before he ever picked up a camera — nine years of stage choreography before a friend's wedding put a camera in his hands almost by accident. What he noticed first wasn't the light or the lens. It was that every photographer at that wedding was fighting the same problem: nobody could predict where anyone was going to stand.

So he started doing both. First informally, choreographing a friend's sangeet the same week he shot another friend's wedding. Then, in 2019, as an actual business — one studio, one point of contact, one shared plan for how the day would move and how it would be captured.

Today the studio is a small team of photographers, one associate choreographer, and a film editor — but every wedding still starts with Kabir walking the venue, camera in one hand, counting steps with the other.

How we work

What doesn't change from wedding to wedding

01

The plan comes before the camera

Every wedding gets a shared run sheet — choreography counts and shot list on the same page, so nothing is guessed on the day.

02

Comfort before spectacle

We choreograph to what a family can actually do well, not to what looks impressive on Instagram. It shows in the photos.

03

You see everything, unfiltered first

Culled galleries within 10 days, full edited sets within 6 weeks. No placeholder previews with logos stamped across your own wedding.

Studio timeline

From rehearsal rooms to reception floors

2015

Started choreographing sangeet performances

Freelance choreography for friends' weddings around Bengaluru, alongside a full-time role in stage production.

2018

Picked up a camera, kept the counts

Started shooting weddings alongside choreography bookings — and noticed how much easier both jobs got when done together.

2019

The Knot & Narratives founded

Formalised as a two-service studio: wedding photography and wedding choreography, booked together or separately.

2022

First destination wedding, first international booking

Grew the crew to include a second photographer, an associate choreographer, and a dedicated film editor.

2026

240+ weddings, 9 countries

Still walking every venue in person before the wedding. Still counting steps out loud. Some things don't scale, on purpose.
